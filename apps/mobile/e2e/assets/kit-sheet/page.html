Cooler kit for single-board computers

Fitting sheet, kit CK-5

In the box

  1. Heatsink with fan and fan cable
  2. Two spring-loaded push pins (fitted)
  3. Thermal pads (fitted, with backing film)
Power off the board and unplug its power cable before you start.

Fit the cooler

  1. Peel the backing film off the thermal pads.
  2. Hold the cooler over the board with the fan above the processor and the pins over the two mounting holes.
  3. Press both pins down at the same time until they click.
Cooler kit CK-51

Connect and check

Connect the fan

  1. Lift the latch on the connector marked FAN.
  2. Push the fan cable in, contacts facing the board, and close the latch.

Check

  1. The cooler sits flat, with no gap at the pads.
  2. Both pins are clipped through the board.
  3. Power on: the fan spins up briefly, then slows.
